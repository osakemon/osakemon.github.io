<p class="research-lead">I develop multimodal foundation models and research tools for biological discovery, connecting tissue morphology with molecular measurements.</p>

I am a **fifth-year PhD candidate** at [Weill Cornell Medicine](https://weill.cornell.edu) and a visiting graduate research fellow at [Houston Methodist](https://www.houstonmethodist.org), advised by [Dr. Guangyu Wang](https://guangyuwanglab.github.io/web/). My research spans **computational pathology, single-cell and spatial biology, multi-omics integration, and dynamical modeling of biological systems**.

I work across model design, data curation, training, evaluation, downstream applications, and software release. My work combines **foundation models, multimodal representation learning, and contrastive learning** with analyses of transcriptomic, proteomic, and metabolomic data to study biology and disease.

Previously, I studied Active Inference for goal-directed migration with [Dr. Naoki Honda](https://sites.google.com/view/data-driven-biology/) during my master's at [Kyoto University](https://www.kyoto-u.ac.jp/en). I hold a bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Technology from [Nanjing University of Posts and Telecommunications](https://www.njupt.edu.cn/en/).
