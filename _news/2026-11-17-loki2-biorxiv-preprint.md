My first-author preprint, "A Biologically Interpretable Single-Cell Pathology Foundation Model for Molecular Decoding of H&E Images," is now available on **bioRxiv**. We introduce Loki2 and the UniSeg resource of 15 million cells to study what molecular information can be recovered from routine histology. [Read the preprint](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.11.14.766416v1).
