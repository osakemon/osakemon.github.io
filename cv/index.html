Profile

Machine-learning researcher developing multimodal foundation models and research tools for computational pathology, single-cell biology, spatial biology, dynamical modeling of biological systems, and multi-omics analysis, including transcriptomics, proteomics, and metabolomics. Led end-to-end development of OmiCLIP, a visual-omics foundation model, and its downstream platform, Loki (Nature Methods), from model design and data curation through training, evaluation, and software release, including downstream task development and applications to biological research. International and interdisciplinary background: trained in computer science in China, theoretical biology in Japan, and AI for biomedicine in the United States. First and co-first author of work published in Nature Methods, Nature Communications, and Circulation Research, with a first-author manuscript in third-round revision at Nature.

Research & engineering experience

Houston Methodist Research Institute / Weill Cornell Medicine

Aug. 2022 – Present

Visiting Graduate Research Fellow | Houston, TX

  • Led OmiCLIP, a visual-omics foundation model, from model design and data curation through training and evaluation, aligning histopathology with transcriptomics via contrastive learning on 2.2 million paired tissue patches from 1,007 samples across 32 organs, curated from 113 studies. Benchmarked the model and its downstream platform, Loki, against 22 state-of-the-art methods across five core downstream task categories; co-first author, Nature Methods (2025).
  • Built and released Loki, the open-source downstream platform that translates OmiCLIP into five capabilities for biological research: tissue alignment, molecular annotation, cell-type decomposition, image-transcriptomics retrieval, and spatial gene-expression prediction from H&E images. Across visual-omics projects and Loki2, worked with TB-scale pathology datasets spanning over 100,000 images. Code · Model weights · Project page
  • Led end-to-end development of a single-cell pathology foundation model for molecular decoding of H&E images, spanning model design, data curation, training, evaluation, software development, downstream task development, and applications to biological research; first-author manuscript in third-round revision at Nature.
  • Led downstream application design and analysis for Thor, integrating histology and spatial transcriptomics at cellular resolution to enable CNV-based tumor mapping in ductal carcinoma in situ and identify vascular PLA2G2A enrichment in post-LVAD heart tissues; co-first author, Nature Communications (2025). Code · Project page
  • Collaborative biological discovery: Computational analyses with experimental teams linked LATS1/2-CD38 signaling to endothelial senescence and thrombosis, mTORC1 and AIBP-VEGFR3 to lymphatic development, and metastatic precursor cells to candidate HSP90AA1/HSP90AB1 biomarkers; co-first author, Circulation Research; contributing author, Developmental Cell and Nature Communications.
  • Technical mentoring and training: Mentored five junior Ph.D. students, rotation students, and research assistants through shared pipelines, code review, and result reproduction; trainee analyses supported downstream applications and one trainee built an independent pipeline. Contributed materials and training for the ISMB 2026 tutorial on visual-omics foundation models; coordinated compute needs with HPC platform providers.
